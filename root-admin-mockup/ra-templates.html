Site templates

A template decides how a storefront looks and is laid out. It cannot change prices, stock, policies, validation or checkout behaviour — so two clients on this platform never have to look alike, and a store can change template without touching its data.

Versions and assignment

What a template may change

Layout and section order · component styling and density · typography and spacing · imagery treatment · motion · which presentational sections exist · how a section is arranged on small screens

What a template may never change

Prices or how they are calculated · stock and availability statements · tax · policies and disclosures · validation · checkout steps and their rules · which features exist · a brand name, colour or wording, which all come from the store's own configuration
Because every template renders the same data through the same contracts, switching one store from Forge to Aurora needs no data migration and can be reversed in one step. Adding a template later does not require a separate application for any store.
