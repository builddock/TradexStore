    Stacking policy (rule set v2026.09.3)

    • Max one coupon per order.
    • Automatic promotions + coupon only if both are marked combinable.
    • Consumer promotions never apply on top of dealer lists or contracts.
    • Dealer promotions apply after the quantity tier, only when flagged.
    • Bank / payment offers are applied by the provider and itemised separately.
    • If two exclusive promotions qualify, the higher-priority rule wins — not automatically the larger discount.
