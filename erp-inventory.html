Stale-feed handling

Per-supplier freshness deadline (default 24 h)
Fresh (< 24 h)Offer shows supplier lead time; can be promised at checkout with buffer applied.
Stale (24–48 h)Hide immediate-delivery promise · “Confirm availability” — order held until the supplier confirms.
Expired (> 48 h)Offer suspended from sale · vendor and buyer notified · reactivates on next valid feed.
