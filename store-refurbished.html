How refurbishment works

Six steps before a unit can be listed — and each one is recorded against its serial number

01Source

Corporate lease returns and approved refurbishers. Purchase source and ownership proof recorded; serials checked before intake.

0242-point inspection

Display, keyboard, ports, battery, thermals, storage health, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, camera, mic, speakers and hinges.

03Secure data wipe

Storage erased with certified software (NIST 800-88). Genuine OS reinstalled clean. Wipe certificate issued.

04Grading

Graded Open box, A, B or Used against a written standard. High-value units get a second check.

05Unit photography

At least 6 photos of the actual unit — including every mark bigger than 5 mm.

06Warranty

6-month Tradex warranty (3 months for Used), registered to your serial when you buy.

Units that fail inspection, can't be wiped securely, or are locked (iCloud/BIOS) are never listed — they go back to the supplier or to an authorised recycler. About 1 in 6 units we receive doesn't make it to the store.

Refurbished FAQ

More answers →
Is refurbished the same as used?

No. “Used · Good” units are tested and sold as-is with described wear. Refurbished units (Grade A/B) are cleaned, repaired where needed, fully inspected, wiped and graded to a written standard.

Who provides the warranty?

Tradex Electronics — it's our seller warranty, not the manufacturer's. It's shown on every listing and your invoice. Open-box items keep any remaining manufacturer warranty.

Were any parts replaced?

If we replace a part (e.g. battery, keyboard, SSD), the listing says which part and whether it's original or a certified compatible part.

Can I see the unit before buying?

Yes — reserve it online and view it at SP Road, Koramangala or Mysuru. Reservations for viewing are held for 24 hours.

Do refurbished laptops come with Windows?

Yes, a genuine licence (usually Windows 11 Pro from the original device), clean-installed. Microsoft Office is not included unless the listing says so.

Can businesses buy refurbished in bulk?

Approved dealers can order refurbished units by SKU at tier prices, or request a quote for a matched lot with unit-level reports. Dealer zone

Longer device life

Keeping good devices in use

Buying refurbished gives a working device a second life instead of a new one being made. Parts we can't reuse go to an authorised e-waste recycler under India's E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022.

We don't publish carbon-saving numbers we can't measure for each product.
