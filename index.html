What changed after the client review

Three change requests from the review of v0.1, applied without removing any existing feature.

1 · Original storefront design

A new “Studio” identity replaces the marketplace look: new header and department rail, bento home page, redesigned product cards, a two-column product page with one purchase panel, and an editorial footer.

2 · Team & activity monitor

For the owner: each employee's live status and current work, their activity and sensitive actions, and how every area of the workspace is being handled. Work activity only — no screen, keystroke or location tracking.

Open Team monitor →
3 · Analytics dashboard

Sales, product, customer and store analytics with one filter row: trends, channels, locations, top products, margins, cohorts, segments, the store funnel and operations measures.

How to review this prototype

The dark toolbar at the top of every screen is part of the prototype, not the product.

View as Guest / Consumer / Dealer

On storefront screens, switch buyer context to see public prices, GST-inclusive consumer prices, or a dealer's private ex-GST price list with 5+ and 10+ quantity tiers.

Phase notes

Turn on to label each feature with its delivery phase (1A, 1B, 2, 3) and requirement ID (R01–R20) from the requirements blueprint.

ⓘ Help & guides

In the ERP workspace and vendor portal, click any ⓘ or the ? button for a plain-language explanation; dotted-underlined short names explain themselves. Key ERP pages open with a “How it works” guide.

All screens

Jump to any of the 34 screens across the three applications. Tabs, drawers, modals, cart, compare and chat are interactive.

Sample data only

Products, prices, people, orders and vendors are illustrative. Product photos are free-licence stock images (see credits). Real catalogue and brand assets come from discovery.

Delivery phases

Phase 1 = e-commerce web application + ERP web application. Mobile-friendly e-commerce and the mobile app are Phase 2.

PHASE 0

Discovery & proof

Process maps, current-system audit, this UI prototype, platform fit tests, data sample, backlog and estimate.

PHASE 1A

Core web launch

Consumer & dealer storefront, ERP workspace, central stock & serials, purchasing/receipt, payments, dispatch, basic returns, reports, access control.

PHASE 1B

Operational completion

Controlled vendor portal, validated bulk import, guided WhatsApp ordering, approvals and selected integrations.

PHASE 2

Mobile & approved growth

Mobile-friendly e-commerce and mobile app on Phase 1 APIs. Optional marketplace with seller settlements.

PHASE 3

AI & expansion

Grounded AI assistance, advanced automation, and possible additional storefronts or businesses.

Decisions this prototype assumes

To confirm in the review meeting (blueprint §1.4, §27.2)
“Retail users” = consumers; dealers = approved businesses.
Consumers see GST-inclusive prices; dealers see ex-GST prices with 5+/10+ tiers.
Tradex sells in its own name at launch.
Vendors submit products and stock for approval (1B). Marketplace sellers are Phase 2.
Dealer orders are prepaid.
Credit limits, ageing and collections are not shown.
One payment gateway and one courier at launch
COD depends on eligibility rules.
Existing accounting system stays the ledger.
The ERP exports invoices and credits with control totals.
Approval thresholds and return windows are placeholders.
The client supplies the real values.
Branch and store names are illustrative.
Assumed locations: SP Road, Koramangala, Mysuru and a Central Warehouse.

Not in Phase 1 (by design)

Mobile app and mobile-specific optimisation, AI/LLM chat, marketplace payouts, automated collection calls, full accounting replacement, payroll/HR and a general workflow builder. These features appear in the prototype only as locked previews or phase-tagged notes.
