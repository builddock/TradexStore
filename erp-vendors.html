Performance score

Scores drive vendor priority in replenishment suggestions and whether routine stock refreshes are auto-accepted. They never change prices on their own.
Score weights (proposed)
Fill rate 30% · response time 20% · return/defect 20% · first-pass data quality 15% · feed on time 15%. Below 70 → buyer review of permissions; below 60 → auto-accept of stock refreshes switched off.
