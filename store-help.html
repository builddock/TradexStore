Orders & tracking

Why does my order show “paid” but not “dispatched”?
Payment, packing and delivery are tracked separately. After payment is confirmed we pick, test (for refurbished units) and pack your item — usually the same day if paid before 2 PM. You'll get a message with the courier tracking number at dispatch. Track your order
My order arrived in two parts. Is something missing?
Items stocked at different locations may ship separately. Each shipment has its own tracking number and invoice. Your order page shows “1 of 2 shipments dispatched” until everything is on its way.
Can I cancel an order?
Yes, until it's packed. Open the order and choose Cancel; the refund starts immediately. After dispatch, refuse the delivery or request a return once it arrives.
Why was my order cancelled by Tradex?
Rarely, a unit fails our pre-dispatch check or the last unit sells in a store at the same moment. We cancel only that item, tell you why, and refund in full — we never substitute a different condition or configuration without asking.

Payments & refunds

My payment was deducted but the order says “awaiting payment”.
Some UPI and net-banking payments take a few minutes to confirm. Don't pay again. We check with the bank automatically; if it isn't confirmed within 30 minutes, any deducted amount is refunded by your bank in 5–7 working days.
How long do refunds take?
After the returned item passes inspection: UPI 1–3 working days, cards and net banking 5–7 working days, cash-on-delivery orders by bank transfer (NEFT) within 3 working days of receiving your account details. You'll receive a credit note for every refund.
Is no-cost EMI available?
On most items above ₹10,000 with major credit cards and selected debit cards (3, 6 or 9 months). The bank's interest is given to you as an upfront discount; the bank may charge GST on that interest.
Can I get a GST invoice with my business GSTIN?
Yes — add your GSTIN at checkout before paying; it can't be added after the invoice is issued. Regular business buyers should apply for a dealer account.

Returns & warranty

How do I return or replace an item?
Go to Returns & warranty, choose the item and reason, and book a free pickup or drop it at a store. Serial-numbered items are matched to the exact unit we sold you.
Why isn't my refund issued as soon as the courier collects the item?
Returned items first go to a separate quarantine area and are inspected (usually within 2 working days of arrival). This confirms it's the same unit and condition, and protects your data. Then the refund is released.
Who do I contact for a warranty repair?
Start with us for anything bought from Tradex. For Tradex (seller) warranty we repair or replace ourselves; for manufacturer warranty we guide you to the brand's service centre or send it on your behalf. See warranty explained.

Refurbished & used

What's the difference between Grade A and Grade B?
Both pass the same 42-point functional inspection. Grade A shows minimal signs of use and has battery health of at least 85%; Grade B has light visible wear and at least 75% battery health. See the full grading standard.
Will I receive the exact unit in the photos?
For listings marked “Unit photos”, yes — the photos, serial (partly masked) and inspection report belong to that unit. If it sells before you check out, we show you the next unit's own report before you pay.
Is my data safe on refurbished devices?
Every storage device is wiped with a certified erasure tool before resale, and a wipe certificate is linked to the unit. Returned devices are wiped again before they're resold.

Warranty explained

Every product page shows who provides the warranty. A manufacturer warranty is honoured by the brand; a Tradex warranty is our own promise as the seller.

Manufacturer warranty
New and open-box products
  • Provided by the brand (e.g. Dell, Sony, Canon) — 1 to 3 years
  • Repairs at the brand's authorised service centre
  • Needs your GST invoice — download it from your account
  • We help you raise the claim, or send it on your behalf from any Tradex store
Tradex warranty
Refurbished, used and Tradex builds
  • Provided by Tradex Electronics — 3 or 6 months; 3 years on Tradex custom builds
  • Repaired at our SP Road service desk, or replaced with an equal unit
  • Covers hardware faults in normal use
  • Warranty travels with the serial — replacement units keep the remaining period

Cash on delivery (COD)

  • Available for orders from ₹500 to ₹50,000 at serviceable PIN codes. A ₹49 COD fee applies.
  • Not available for dealer orders, custom builds, pre-orders or partner-stock items.
  • We confirm COD orders by a one-time code to your phone before dispatch.
  • If COD deliveries are refused twice without reason, COD is paused on the account for 90 days.

If delivery fails

The courier tries 3 times. After that the parcel returns to us (“return to origin”) and we refund prepaid orders in full once it's back — usually within 7 days. Opened, damaged or tampered parcels: refuse delivery and tell us within 48 hours with photos.
