Record-scope notes

Who sees which records and fields
  • Branch managers & staff — orders, stock and customers of their assigned location; company-wide availability is read-only (reserved and in-transit shown separately).
  • Warehouse — tasks for their site; no prices or customer phone beyond the shipping label.
  • Support — verified order lookup; phone and address masked until the customer is verified; never payment instrument details.
  • Finance — payments, refunds and settlements for all locations; personal data only for a finance purpose.
  • Vendors — only their own products, offers, POs, returns and statements. Object-level checks on every request (tested by changing IDs, §19.1).
  • Sensitive fields — supplier cost, margin, bank accounts, KYC documents: hidden unless the role allows; every view of a KYC file is logged.
