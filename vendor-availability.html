How your availability is used

Policy
  • Supplier stock is never counted as Tradex stock. Customers see it only as “Ships in 4–6 days · partner stock” — never “In stock”.
  • It becomes Tradex stock only after goods are received and inspected at a Tradex warehouse (GRN + QC).
  • Tradex holds back a safety buffer of 2 units per offer before promising delivery.
  • Quantities you confirm on a PO are deducted from your declared availability automatically.
  • Routine refreshes within ±50% are accepted automatically; bigger jumps are held for a quick Tradex check.
  • If the feed isn't refreshed in 24 h, delivery promises pause automatically; after 72 h offers are hidden.
