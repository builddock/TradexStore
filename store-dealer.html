How quantity tiers work

Tiers apply per SKU, per order line, to all units on that line. Order 10 of one SKU and every unit gets the 10+ price.

  • Dealer prices are shown excluding GST; GST is added at checkout on your invoice.
  • Change the quantity and the tier is recalculated before you pay.
  • If a return drops a line below its tier, we explain any adjustment before refunding.
  • Promotions and dealer prices don't stack unless the promotion says so.
  • Very large or mixed quantities? Request a quote for a locked price.
Dealer prices are private. Guests and consumers see public prices only; dealer prices are never shown in public pages, search results or shared caches.

Frequently asked questions

Who can apply for dealer pricing?

Retailers, resellers, system integrators, service centres and companies/institutions buying for their own use. You need an active GST registration in India. Individuals and unregistered businesses can buy at public prices and still get a GST invoice.

Why do you verify my GSTIN? I typed it correctly.

A typed number could belong to someone else. We check it against the GST portal (active status, legal name, state) and match it with your PAN and documents. Dealer prices are only switched on after this check.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum order value. Tier prices start at 5 units of the same SKU. Some refurbished lots are sold only by quote.

Can I buy on credit?

Dealer orders are prepaid by default (UPI, net banking, NEFT/RTGS, cards). Credit terms can be requested after 6 months of trading and need finance approval.

Can several people from my business use the account?

Yes. The owner invites members as Buyers (can order, optional per-order limit) or Accounts (invoices and statements only). Each person signs in with their own phone or email.

Do returns and warranty work differently for dealers?

The same return windows and warranty apply. Dealers get a bulk RMA option for multiple units and a named contact for escalations.

What happens when my approval expires?

Price lists are reviewed yearly. We remind you 30 days before; if documents aren't renewed, dealer pricing pauses but your account and history stay.

Ready to apply?

Keep your GSTIN, PAN and trade licence handy. Most businesses finish in about 3 minutes.
