    What Tradex records about staff activity

    The same notice staff see at their first sign-in and in their profile · wording TBC

    Recorded

    • Actions taken in Tradex — who did what, to which record, when, and from which device and branch network. These are the same entries as the audit log.
    • Sign-ins and sessions — time, device type and browser, the branch or network label, the sign-in method and any failed attempts.
    • Workspace presence — active or idle, worked out only from activity inside the Tradex workspace. Staff can set themselves “On break” or “In a meeting / away”.
    • Work items — when an item was assigned, picked up and finished, so handling times and targets can be measured.

    Not recorded

    • Screen contents or screenshots
    • Keystrokes, typing or mouse movements
    • Webcam or microphone
    • Personal devices, other apps or websites
    • GPS or physical location — only the branch or network label at sign-in
    • Private messages outside Tradex (personal WhatsApp, calls, e-mail)

    How staff are told

    Every staff member sees a short notice at their first sign-in and can reread it any time in My profile → Privacy notice. They can also see their own activity and presence history in My profile → My activity. Notice text TBC — to be drafted with the client’s legal adviser.

    Who can see this page

    The owner. Others only if the owner delegates it — for example a branch manager for their own branch only. Opening a person’s details is itself recorded in the audit log. The owner’s own actions are recorded in the audit log in the same way.

    How long it is kept

    Presence and session details 90 days TBC · performance summaries 24 months TBC · actions stay in the audit log for its retention period (8 years TBC).

    What it is used for

    Running the business fairly: workload and cover, service targets, security, and reviewing sensitive actions. Alerts come from fixed rules, not AI, and a person always decides what happens next — nothing about pay, warnings or dismissal is decided automatically.

    Legal review TBC: the notice, the purpose and the retention periods are to be checked against India’s DPDP Act and rules, and employment law, with the client’s legal adviser before go-live.
