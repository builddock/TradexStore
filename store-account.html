Your profile, login, addresses, wishlist, alerts and marketing preferences. You'll be signed out everywhere.

Tax invoices and credit notes (8 years, GST law), warranty/serial records until the warranty ends, and payment/refund records. These are kept separately with restricted access and are not used for marketing.
